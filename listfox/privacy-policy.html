Privacy Policy for ListFox

ListFox – Notes & Lists, an Android app by FluffyTail Apps.
Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use ListFox. In short: your notes and lists stay on your phone. ListFox has no accounts and no servers of its own. The app shows ads provided by Google, and Google may process some device information for that purpose, as described below.

1. Your notes, lists and settings

  • Everything you write in ListFox (notes, lists, items, dates, reminders, folders, templates and settings) is stored only on your device. FluffyTail Apps never receives it.
  • Android backup: if Android's backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include ListFox's data in your personal Google account backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google under your Google account; FluffyTail Apps cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.
  • Backup files: "Export backup" saves a file to a location you choose (for example your Downloads folder or a cloud drive you use). You decide where it goes and who can access it.
  • Sharing: when you use "Share", the text of that note or list is passed to the app you pick (for example a messaging app). That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device by Android. No reminder data leaves your phone.
  • Voice input: when you tap the microphone ("Voice list", "Voice note", "Dictate"), ListFox opens your phone's own speech recognition (usually Google's). That service listens and turns your speech into text under its own privacy policy (it may process audio online, depending on your phone). ListFox does not access the microphone or receive any audio: it only receives the resulting text, which is stored like anything else you type.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ListFox shows one banner ad on its main screen, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data is processed by Google, not by FluffyTail Apps. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad is shown, ListFox asks for your consent using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Menu → Privacy & ad settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Remove ads: after the one-time "Remove ads" purchase, ListFox no longer shows ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play)

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy. ListFox only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase belongs to your Google account; FluffyTail Apps does not receive your payment details.

4. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in, no analytics tools, no tracking by FluffyTail Apps.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not collect your notes or lists.
  • Apart from Google's ad and purchase services, ListFox does not connect to the internet.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications – to show reminders you set.
  • Alarms & reminders – so reminders arrive exactly on time.
  • Run at startup – to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Keep awake / background tasks – short background work, for example updating your home-screen widgets.
  • Internet, network state, advertising ID and Android ad services (Privacy Sandbox topics and attribution, where available) – used by Google's ad services.
  • Google Play billing – for the optional "Remove ads" purchase.

6. Children

ListFox is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete individual notes and lists in the app at any time. Uninstalling ListFox removes all of its data from your device (backup files you exported yourself and Android backups in your Google account are kept until you delete them).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
